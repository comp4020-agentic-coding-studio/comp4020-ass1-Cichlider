# Assignment 1 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough came when I asked Claude to stop scattering the exploded
parts around the case and instead line them up in a single row, like exhibits
in a museum. The moment that landed, the effect was immediately obvious on
screen, and I felt it right away: this was the layout I actually wanted. Up to
that point I'd been iterating on small visual tweaks without a clear sense of
where the design should go; that one change gave the whole piece a direction
--- every part getting its own clear, ordered spot to be looked at --- that
everything after it (camera framing, the detail panel, the part-to-part
navigation) could build on.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

It sharpened what I want a product I build to feel like: vertical and
intuitive, not a pile of scattered features. More importantly, it changed a
question I now ask myself before building anything: is what I'm making
something nobody else has done, or something that no existing combination of
tools could already replace? That question is really about the necessity of
the work --- not "can this be built" but "should this exist as its own thing."
I want that question to be part of how I scope every project from here on,
not just this one.
